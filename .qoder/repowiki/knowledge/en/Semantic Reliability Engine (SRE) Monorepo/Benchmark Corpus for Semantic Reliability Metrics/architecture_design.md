The corpus is organized as a flat collection of metric directories split into two tracks: `dev/` (actively iterated) and `holdout/` (immutable frozen set). Each metric directory follows a fixed five-file layout: `model_*.sql` defines the metric query against a fixture CSV; `schema.yml` declares column-level dbt-style `not_null` tests; `contract.yaml` documents the metric name, owner, grain, invariants (population filters, aggregation rules, units), and embedded SQL; `semantic_assertions.yaml` specifies runtime checks such as `not_null`, `required_population`, and `metric_value` with tolerances or min/max bounds; and one or more `.csv` files provide deterministic fixture data. The `holdout/holdout_protocol.yaml` pins the frozen track to a specific commit/tag and versions for validity policy, mutation operator, assertion registry, and fixture adequacy, ensuring reproducible benchmark runs across environments.
